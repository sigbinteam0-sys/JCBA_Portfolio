ABOUT US

Porsche is a German luxury and high-performance automobile manufacturer known for iconic sports cars, SUVs, and sedans.

Porsche, officially Dr. Ing. h.c. F. Porsche AG, was founded in 1931 by Ferdinand Porsche in Stuttgart, Germany, alongside partners Adolf Rosenberger and Anton Piëch. The company initially designed vehicles for other manufacturers, most notably creating the Volkswagen Beetle. After World War II, Ferdinands son, Ferry Porsche, developed the Porsche 356, marking the beginning of Porsches own sports car legacy The brand has since become synonymous with precision engineering, performance, and innovation.
